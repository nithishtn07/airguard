Eight Disciplines in One Platform

Traditional monitors display only static historical numbers. AirGuard AI brings continuous real-time data ingestion together with machine learning and automated risk reasoning.

01 / TELEMETRY
Real-Time AQI
Sub-hourly particulate and gaseous air quality readings computed via Indian NAAQS standards.
02 / ATMOSPHERE
Weather Factors
Ambient temperature, relative humidity, wind velocity, direction, pressure, and precipitation.
03 / INGESTION
Historical Database
Continuous SQLite storage enforcing zero lookahead bias with automated integrity and outlier audits.
04 / MACHINE LEARNING
Predictive ML
Random Forest Regressor forecasting next-hour AQI with uncertainty confidence spreads.
05 / PERSISTENCE
Hotspot Detection
Identifies persistent high-pollution regional corridors based on multi-hour rolling thresholds.
06 / CAUSALITY
Anomaly Engine
Detects sudden statistical spikes ($|z| \ge 2.5$) departing from historical baseline distributions.
07 / MULTI-FACTOR
Risk Assessment
Composite 0–100 risk scoring synthesizing AQI, PM2.5, hotspot duration, and anomaly severity.
08 / ACTIONABLE
Smart Advisories
Contextual guidance for sensitive groups, outdoor activities, travel, and indoor ventilation.
End-to-End Pipeline

How AirGuard AI Processes Intelligence

From raw sensor telemetry to preventative health advisories in seconds.

STEP 1
Live Telemetry
Real-time Open-Meteo & atmospheric sensors ingest PM2.5, PM10, gases & weather.
STEP 2
Preprocessing
Outlier filtering, causal SQLite persistence, and rolling lag feature creation.
STEP 3
ML Inference
Random Forest ensemble evaluates weather-pollution interactions and temporal trends.
STEP 4
Forecast
Generates 1-hour ahead predicted AQI, mathematical trend vectors & bounds.
STEP 5
Risk Scoring
Combines pollution magnitude, hotspot persistence, and anomaly spike Z-scores.
STEP 6
Advisories
Dispatches categorized guidance, alert notifications, and geospatial map updates.

Predictive Science

Why Forecasting Matters

Traditional air quality apps inform you that the air was bad an hour ago. AirGuard AI analyzes current atmospheric telemetry and historical patterns to anticipate whether pollution will worsen over the coming hour, allowing proactive protection.

01 / IS IT SAFE?

Evaluates current particulate exposure against Indian NAAQS and WHO guidelines.

02 / IS IT RISING?

Forecasts pollution velocity and direction vectors to preempt dangerous spikes.

03 / IS IT A HOTSPOT?

Differentiates transient traffic plumes from prolonged regional stagnation.

04 / WHAT TO DO?

Delivers contextual precautions tailored to vulnerable groups, outdoor sports, and travel.
