Why is an Area Flagged as a Hotspot?

01 / POLLUTION MAGNITUDE

Evaluates how far sub-hourly AQI exceeds the Indian NAAQS threshold of 100 AQI, scaling continuously up to severe hazard levels.

02 / PERSISTENCE DURATION

Differentiates momentary traffic spikes from stagnation where air remains degraded for over 6 to 24 consecutive hours.

03 / PARTICULATE CONCENTRATION

Heavily weights PM2.5 and PM10 particulate concentrations due to proven deep pulmonary and cardiovascular health impacts.
